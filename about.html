About DJ Tavik

From the Bylanes of Bangalore to the World Stage

Born amid the incense and cassette shops of Bangalore, Tavik grew up with tabla lessons in one ear and pirated house records in the other. That collision became a calling: to fuse the emotion of Indian classical music with the pulse of the global dancefloor.

Over fifteen years, Tavik has turned that vision into a signature sound: sitar hooks over rolling basslines, Bollywood samples reborn as techno anthems, and mandala-drenched visual shows that turn every set into a ceremony.

Away from the decks, the artist behind DJ Tavik lives a striking double life. By day, they are a Security Malware Researcher Engineer at Microsoft, hunting down threats and reverse-engineering malicious code to help keep millions of users safe.

🛡️

By Day: Microsoft Malware Researcher Engineer - II

As a Security Malware Researcher Engineer, Tavik dissects malware, tracks emerging cyber threats and builds detections that protect people at global scale. The same precision, pattern-hunting and late-night focus that goes into decoding a threat goes into crafting a flawless set, two very different stages, one relentless attention to detail.
